DupeShots - Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

Overview

DupeShots is a photo library cleaner for iPhone. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how DupeShots handles your data.

Data collection

DupeShots does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising frameworks. No account or sign-up is required.

Photo access

DupeShots requests access to your photo library solely to scan for similar photos and help you free up storage. All photo processing happens entirely on your device using Apple's built-in Photos and Vision frameworks. Your photos never leave your phone.

In-app purchases

DupeShots offers a one-time in-app purchase to unlock unlimited deletions. This transaction is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store any payment information.

Third-party services

DupeShots does not integrate with any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs.

Children's privacy

DupeShots does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
